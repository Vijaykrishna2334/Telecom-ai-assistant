# JioAirFiber Plans (January 2025)

JioAirFiber is a 5G-based fixed wireless broadband service. No wires to your home - uses a 5G outdoor antenna.

## Standard AirFiber Plans
| Plan | Speed | Data (FUP) | Price/Month | Voice | OTT Apps |
|------|-------|------------|-------------|-------|----------|
| AirFiber Base | 30 Mbps | 1000 GB | ₹599 | Free (Jio Call app) | Basic Jio apps |
| AirFiber Standard | 100 Mbps | 1000 GB | ₹899 | Free | 800+ TV channels |
| AirFiber Plus | 100 Mbps | 1000 GB | ₹1,199 | Free | Netflix, Prime Lite, 15+ OTT |

## AirFiber Max Plans (Higher Speeds)

## Installation
- **Installation Fee:** ₹1,000 (waived on annual plan)
- **Booking Amount:** ₹100 (adjusted in bill)
- **Free Equipment:**
  - Outdoor 5G Antenna (ODU)
  - Indoor WiFi Router
  - 4K Set-top Box
  - Voice Remote

## Availability
- Requires **Jio 5G coverage** in your area
- Check availability: jio.com/airfiber or call 1800-88-99999
- Book via MyJio app or Jio Store

## JioAirFiber vs JioFiber
| Feature | JioAirFiber | JioFiber |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Connection Type | 5G Wireless | Fiber Optic Cable |
| Installation Time | Same day possible | 2-3 days |
| Speed Stability | Good (weather can affect) | Excellent (stable) |
| Availability | 5G areas only | Fiber-covered areas |
| Data FUP | 1000 GB | 3300 GB |

## After FUP Data
- Speed reduced after 1000 GB
- Can buy data add-on packs:
  - ₹101 for 10 GB
  - ₹201 for 20 GB
