## Postpaid Benefits
- **No daily limits** - Use full data anytime
- **Data rollover** - Unused data carries to next month (up to 200 GB)
- **Unlimited 5G** - Truly unlimited in 5G areas
- **International roaming** - Included in some plans
- **Credit limit** - No need to recharge before travel

## How to Switch to Postpaid
1. Open MyJio App
2. Go to "Upgrade to Postpaid"
3. Select plan
4. Complete payment
5. SIM converts instantly
