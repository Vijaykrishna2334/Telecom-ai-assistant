## 🔄 Switch to Postpaid

### How to Upgrade from Prepaid
1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **"Upgrade to Postpaid"**
3. Select your preferred plan
4. Complete KYC verification
5. SIM converts **instantly** - no new SIM needed!

### Documents Required
- Aadhaar Card (for eKYC)
- PAN Card (for credit check)
- Address proof (if different from Aadhaar)

### Credit Check
- Instant approval for existing Jio users
- Credit limit: ₹500 - ₹10,000 based on usage
- No security deposit for most plans
