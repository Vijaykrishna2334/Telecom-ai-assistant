## 5G Unlimited Data
- All plans ₹239 and above with 2 GB/day or more include **truly unlimited 5G data**
- 5G data doesn't count against your 4G quota
- Requires 5G-enabled device and area with 5G coverage

## How to Recharge
1. **MyJio App** - Download from Play Store/App Store
2. **Jio.com** - Visit website, enter number, select plan
3. **UPI/Paytm/PhonePe** - Enter Jio number, pay
4. **Jio Store** - Visit nearest store

## Check Balance & Validity
- Dial **\*333#** from your Jio number
- Open **MyJio App** → Home → View Balance
- SMS **"BAL"** to **199**
