## 🌍 How It Works

### What is Wi-Fi Calling?

Wi-Fi Calling (VoWiFi) lets you make calls using your Jio number through any WiFi network instead of cellular towers.

### Benefits

✅ **No roaming charges** - Use hotel/cafe WiFi  
✅ **Same Jio number** - No need for local SIM  
✅ **Crystal clear quality** - HD voice calls  
✅ **Works globally** - Any country with WiFi  
✅ **Receive calls FREE** - People can call your Jio number

## 🔧 How to Enable Wi-Fi Calling

### Step 1: Enable on Phone (Before Travel)

**For Android:**
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Navigate to **SIM & Network** or **Connections**
3. Find **Wi-Fi Calling** or **VoWiFi**
4. Turn **ON**

**For iPhone:**
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Phone** or **Cellular**
3. Find **Wi-Fi Calling**
4. Turn **ON**

### Step 2: Buy WiFi Calling Pack

1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **Recharge** → **International** → **Wi-Fi Calling**
3. Choose pack (₹121 or ₹521)
4. Complete payment
5. Pack activates immediately

### Step 3: Use Abroad

1. Connect to **any WiFi network** (hotel, airport, cafe)
2. Make calls normally using your phone's dialer
3. Calls route through WiFi automatically
4. Your Jio number shows on recipient's screen

## ❓ Common Questions

### Can I receive calls?
**Yes!** Incoming calls are **FREE** when connected to WiFi.

### Do I need internet?
**Yes**, you need a WiFi connection. Mobile data won't work.

### Which countries work?
**All countries** with WiFi networks (170+ countries).

### Can I call India?
**Yes**, you can call India or any country using your minutes.

### What if WiFi quality is poor?
Call may drop or quality may reduce. Try switching to a better WiFi network.
