## 📱 How to Book JioFiber - Apply Online, Check Availability

**How to get JioFiber connection?** Steps to book online, check availability in your area, and schedule installation.

### Online Booking
1. Visit **jio.com/fiber**
2. Enter your **address/PIN code**
3. Check **availability**
4. Select plan and pay ₹100 booking amount
5. Schedule installation slot

### Via MyJio App
1. Download **MyJio App**
2. Go to **JioFiber** section
3. Enter address and check availability
4. Complete booking
