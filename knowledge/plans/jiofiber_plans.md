## Installation
- **Installation Fee:** ₹1,000 (waived on annual plans)
- **Booking Fee:** ₹100 (adjusted in first bill)
- **Free Equipment:** Jio Home Gateway Router + 4K Set-top Box
- **Time:** Usually 2-3 days after booking

## How to Book JioFiber
1. Visit **jio.com/fiber**
2. Enter your address to check availability
3. Choose a plan and complete payment
4. Technician will visit for installation

## Fair Usage Policy
- All plans have **3,300 GB FUP** per month
- After FUP, speed reduced to 1 Mbps

## Speed Test
- Use **fast.com** or **speedtest.net**
- Connect via LAN cable for accurate results
- 5 GHz WiFi gives better speed than 2.4 GHz
