## 📋 Pay As You Go Rates

### Standard PayGo Rates Notes:
1. CDMA account customers will have to buy suitable International roaming pack to make usage in international destination
2. International Roaming services do not work on JioFi & JioPhone devices
3. Pack benefits are applicable only on the preferred partner network in the visited country. If any other partner is chosen then standard rates will be applicable
4. Calling US toll free numbers starting with 1800, 1833, 1844, 1855, 1866, 1877 & 1888, while on international roaming in US will be charged at local rate of Rs. 2/min
5. International roaming customers calling those numbers from any other country apart from US will be charged at applicable Rest of World (ROW) PayGo Rates

## 🔄 How to Activate International Roaming

1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **Recharge** → **International Roaming**
3. Select your destination country
4. Choose a suitable IR pack
5. Complete payment
6. IR pack activates immediately

### Important Tips:
✅ Activate IR pack **BEFORE leaving India**
✅ Turn on **Data Roaming** on your phone upon arrival
✅ After high-speed data exhausts, speed reduces to 64 Kbps
✅ Receiving SMS is **FREE** even without IR pack
✅ Use **Wi-Fi Calling** to save on roaming charges
