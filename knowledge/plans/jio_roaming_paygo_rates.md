## ⚠️ Important PayGo Notes

1. **CDMA customers** must buy a roaming pack (PayGo not available)
2. **JioFi & JioPhone** devices do NOT support international roaming
3. Pack benefits only apply on **preferred partner networks** in visited country
4. If you choose another network, PayGo rates apply
5. **US toll-free numbers** (1800, 1833, 1844, 1855, 1866, 1877, 1888) from US roaming: **₹2/min**
6. Calling US toll-free from other countries: **ROW PayGo rates** apply

## 📱 How to Check Your PayGo Balance

1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **Usage** → **International Roaming**
3. View **current usage and charges**
4. Activate a pack to avoid high PayGo costs

---

## 🔄 Switch from PayGo to Pack Anytime

You can buy a roaming pack **even while abroad**:
1. Open **MyJio App** (with WiFi/data)
2. Go to **Recharge** → **International Roaming**
3. Select your current country
4. Choose a pack
5. Pack activates immediately
6. Future usage will use pack benefits instead of PayGo
