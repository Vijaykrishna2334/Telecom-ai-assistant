# Billing FAQs

## General Billing Questions

### When is my bill due?
Your bill is due on the same date each month, typically 15-20 days after your billing cycle ends. You can find your specific due date on your monthly bill or in your online account.

### How can I view my bill?
You can view your bill in several ways:
- Online through your account portal
- Mobile app under "Billing"
- Email (if you've signed up for paperless billing)
- Request a paper copy by mail

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- Bank account (ACH)
- PayPal
- In-person at retail locations
- Auto-pay (with discount)

### Can I set up automatic payments?
Yes! Auto-pay is available and gives you a $5 discount per month. You can set it up through:
- Your online account
- Mobile app
- Calling customer service
- At a retail location

## Billing Issues

### My bill is higher than expected. Why?
Common reasons for higher bills:
- Data overage charges
- International calls or roaming
- Premium services or subscriptions
- One-time fees (device purchase, activation)
- Late payment fees
- Mid-cycle plan changes

### What should I do if I can't pay my bill on time?
Contact us immediately if you're having trouble paying:
- We offer payment arrangements
- Extension options may be available
- We want to work with you to avoid service interruption

### How do I dispute a charge?
To dispute a charge:
1. Call customer service at 1-800-TELECOM
2. Have your bill ready with the specific charge
3. Explain why you believe the charge is incorrect
4. We'll investigate and respond within 5-7 business days

### What are late payment fees?
A late fee of $5 is charged if payment is not received by the due date. Service may be interrupted if payment is 30 days overdue.

## Plan Changes and Billing

### When does a plan change affect my bill?
- Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges
- Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle
- You'll see the changes reflected on your next bill

### Do you offer billing credits?
Yes, we may provide credits for:
- Service outages
- Billing errors
- Promotional offers
- Customer service issues
Credits typically appear within 1-2 billing cycles.

## Understanding Your Bill

### What are "one-time charges"?
One-time charges include:
- Device purchases
- Activation fees
- Restocking fees
- International roaming
- Directory assistance
- Paper bill fees

### What is a "prorated charge"?
Prorated charges occur when you:
- Start service mid-cycle
- Change your plan
- Add or remove services
You're only charged for the days you used the service.

### How can I reduce my bill?
Ways to save:
- Enable auto-pay ($5/month discount)
- Switch to paperless billing
- Review and remove unused features
- Consider a lower-tier plan if appropriate
- Take advantage of family plans
- Look for promotional offers
