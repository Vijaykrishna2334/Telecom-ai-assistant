# Jio Billing & Payment FAQs

## Q: How can I pay my Jio bill?
**A:** You can pay through multiple methods:
1. **MyJio App** - Open app → Pay Bill/Recharge
2. **Jio.com** - Visit website → Enter number → Pay
3. **UPI Apps** - Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay
4. **Net Banking** - Most banks supported
5. **Debit/Credit Cards** - Visa, Mastercard, Rupay
6. **Jio Store** - Pay in person at any store

## Q: What is JioAutoPay?
**A:** JioAutoPay automatically recharges your prepaid number or pays your postpaid/fiber bill on time.

**Benefits:**
- Never miss a recharge or bill payment
- Manage multiple Jio numbers
- Change plan anytime
- Uses UPI or bank account

**How to set up:**
1. Open MyJio App
2. Go to JioAutoPay section
3. Link your bank account or UPI ID
4. Select your plan (for prepaid) or enable for postpaid

**When does it charge?**
- 5 days before your current plan expires
- For data plans: when 90% data is consumed

## Q: What happens if I miss my recharge date?
**A:** For prepaid:
- No penalty or late fee
- Your number stays active for 15 days (grace period)
- Outgoing calls/data will stop, but you can receive calls
- New plan starts from recharge date, not expiry date

## Q: How do I check my Jio balance?
**A:** Multiple ways:
- Dial **\*333#** from Jio number
- Open **MyJio App** → Home
- SMS **"BAL"** to **199**
- Call **199** (free)

## Q: How do I check my bill for JioFiber/Postpaid?
**A:** 
1. Open MyJio App
2. Select Fiber/Postpaid tab
3. View current and past bills
4. Download PDF if needed

## Q: Can I get a refund?
**A:** Refund policy:
- Prepaid recharge: **No refund** after activation
- Wrong recharge: Contact 199 within 24 hours
- JioFiber installation issues: Contact customer care

## Q: What payment methods are accepted?
**A:** Jio accepts:
- UPI (all apps)
- Debit Cards
- Credit Cards
- Net Banking (50+ banks)
- Jio wallet/JioPay
- Cash at Jio Store
