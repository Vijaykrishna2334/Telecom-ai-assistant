# Jio General FAQs

## Q: How do I activate a new Jio SIM?
**A:** 
1. Insert SIM in your phone
2. Dial **1977** from another phone (SIM activation helpline)
3. Or visit nearest Jio Store with Aadhaar card
4. Complete eKYC (Aadhaar verification)
5. SIM activates within 2-4 hours

## Q: How do I port my number to Jio (MNP)?
**A:** 
1. SMS **"PORT <your 10-digit number>"** to **1900** from your current SIM
2. Receive Unique Porting Code (UPC) via SMS
3. Visit Jio Store with:
   - UPC code
   - ID proof (Aadhaar/Passport)
   - Passport photo
4. Complete eKYC
5. Porting completes in 3-7 days

## Q: What is Jio's network coverage?
**A:** 
- **4G:** Available in all states, 10,000+ cities
- **5G:** 400+ cities covered (expanding)
- Check coverage: jio.com/coverage

## Q: How do I get unlimited 5G data?
**A:** 
- Recharge with any plan ≥₹239 with 2 GB/day or more
- Use a 5G-enabled phone
- Be in a 5G coverage area
- 5G data is truly unlimited (no FUP)

## Q: Can I use Jio SIM in another country?
**A:** Yes, Jio offers International Roaming:
- Activate via MyJio App before travel
- Buy roaming pack (data + calls)
- Works in 170+ countries
- For help abroad: +91 70188 99999 (WhatsApp)

## Q: How do I block/unblock my Jio SIM?
**A:** 
**To block (lost/stolen):**
- Call **1800 88 99999** immediately
- Or visit Jio Store with ID proof

**To unblock:**
- Visit Jio Store with Aadhaar
- File police complaint if stolen
- Get replacement SIM (₹50 fee)

## Q: What is JioCloud?
**A:** Free cloud storage for Jio users:
- 50 GB free storage
- Backup photos, videos, contacts
- Access from any device
- Download JioCloud app

## Q: How do I transfer data to another Jio number?
**A:** Currently, Jio doesn't allow data transfer between numbers. Each number must have its own recharge.

## Q: What happens when my plan expires?
**A:** 
- You get 15-day grace period
- Can receive calls but cannot make calls
- Data stops working
- Recharge to restore services
- Number deactivated after 90 days of no recharge
