# Jio Google Gemini AI Offer

**Jio is offering complimentary access to Google's One AI Premium (Gemini Advanced) subscription for eligible users.**

## Offer Details
- **Benefit:** 18 months of free Google One AI Premium subscription.
- **Includes:**
    - Access to **Gemini Advanced** (Gemini 1.5 Pro model).
    - **2 TB** of Google Cloud storage (shareable with up to 5 family members).
    - Integration with Google Workspace apps (Docs, Gmail, etc.).
    - Use of Gemini in English and 9 Indian languages.

## Eligibility
- **Who:** Existing and new Jio mobile users.
- **Plan Requirement:** Must have an active **Prepaid or Postpaid plan of ₹349 or above** with unlimited 5G data.
- **Device:** Must be using a 5G smartphone on the Jio True 5G network.
- **Age:** 18 years and above.

## How to Activate
1. Open the **MyJio App**.
2. Go to the **Mobile** section.
3. Look for the **"Jio-Google Gemini Offer"** banner or section.
4. Click on **"Activate Now"** or **"Claim Offer"**.
5. Sign in with your Google account to link the subscription.

## Important Notes
- This is a limited-time offer.
- If you already have a Google One subscription, you may need to manage/cancel it to avail this offer (check terms in app).
- Ensuring your plan remains active is required to continue enjoying the benefits.
