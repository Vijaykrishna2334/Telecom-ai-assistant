## MyJio App Support
1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Tap **JioCare** at bottom
3. Select your issue category
4. Chat with support or raise ticket

## Complaint Escalation Process

### Level 1: Customer Care
- Call 199 or 1800-88-99999
- Use MyJio App chat
- Response time: Within 24 hours

### Level 2: Nodal Officer
If not resolved at Level 1:
- Email: telecom.grievance@jio.com
- Phone: 022-79655519
- Response time: Within 7 days

### Level 3: Appellate Authority
If still not resolved:
- Email: appellate.<region>@jio.com
- Decision within 39 days
- Finally, escalate to TRAI (trai.gov.in)
