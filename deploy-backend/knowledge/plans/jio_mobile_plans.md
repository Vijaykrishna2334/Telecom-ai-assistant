## 📶 5G Eligibility Requirements

**To get "Jio True 5G Unlimited" you need ALL of the following:**

1. **Device Check:** Phone must be 5G SA (Standalone) compatible
2. **Plan Check:** Active plan must be ₹239 or higher (2GB/day threshold recommended)
3. **Location Check:** Must be in a Jio 5G coverage zone
4. **Settings Check:** Toggle "5G Auto" or "5G On" in phone settings

**Important:** Plans below 2GB/day (₹199, ₹209, ₹249) do NOT get unlimited 5G by default. Users must buy a 5G upgrade voucher.

## ⚠️ Regional Restrictions & Nuances

### Jammu & Kashmir (J&K)
- Prepaid connections from other states may **NOT roam automatically** in J&K
- Security restrictions apply in border areas
- Postpaid connection or local SIM may be required

### North East India
- Similar roaming restrictions in border areas
- Check connectivity before travel

### Andhra Pradesh / Telangana
- Special regional OTT packs available (SunNXT, Aha)
- Higher demand for regional language content
