## 📶 JioAirFiber Data Limit (FUP) - 1 TB Limit, Speed After Limit

**What is JioAirFiber data limit?** All AirFiber plans have 1 TB (1000 GB) Fair Usage limit. Speed reduces to 64 Kbps after limit.

### Fair Usage Policy (FUP)
- **All AirFiber plans:** **1,000 GB (1 TB)** per billing cycle
- **After FUP exhausted:** Speed throttled to **64 Kbps**
- This is a **Commercial Usage Policy** to prevent abuse
- Wired JioFiber has **3,300 GB (3.3 TB)** FUP - higher than AirFiber

### Speed Factors
- **5G Signal strength:** Affects actual speed
- **Weather:** Heavy rain may temporarily reduce speed
- **Time of day:** Peak hours may see congestion
- **Distance from tower:** Closer = better speed

### Speed Expectations
