## 🔄 How to Activate ISD Pack

1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **Recharge** → **ISD Packs**
3. Select your **destination country**
4. Choose suitable pack
5. Complete payment
6. Pack activates **immediately**

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- All ISD minute packs have **7-day validity** (except Global Pack: 28 days)
- Minutes are for **outgoing calls from India** to the specified countries
- ISD packs can be used on **prepaid and postpaid** connections
- Multiple packs can be recharged for extended talk time
- Check MyJio App for current rates and availability
