## ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **Incoming calls are FREE** (unlimited) in all packs
2. **Activate BEFORE leaving India** via MyJio App
3. **Turn on Data Roaming** on your phone after arrival
4. After high-speed data exhausts, speed reduces to **64 Kbps**
5. Receiving **SMS is FREE** even without IR pack
6. Calling US toll-free numbers (1800, 1833, 1844, 1855, 1866, 1877, 1888) charged at **₹2/min**

## 🔄 How to Activate USA Roaming

1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **Recharge** → **International Roaming**
3. Select **USA** as destination
4. Choose your pack (₹1,555, ₹2,555, or ₹3,455)
5. Complete payment
6. Pack activates **immediately**
