## 🔄 How to Activate Gulf Roaming

1. Open **MyJio App**
2. Go to **Recharge** → **International Roaming**
3. Select your **destination country** (UAE/Saudi/Kuwait/Bahrain/Turkey)
4. Choose your pack (₹898, ₹1,598, or ₹2,998)
5. Complete payment
6. Pack activates **immediately**

---

## ⚠️ Important Tips

✅ Activate pack **BEFORE leaving India**  
✅ Turn on **Data Roaming** on your phone after arrival  
✅ After high-speed data exhausts, speed reduces to **64 Kbps**  
✅ Receiving **SMS is FREE** even without pack  
✅ Use **Wi-Fi Calling** to save on roaming charges
